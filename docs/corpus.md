# Document Corpus: AI Security and Risk Management

All documents are official NIST publications.

1. **Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)** — General framework for identifying and managing AI risks.  
   https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf

2. **Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework: Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile** — Risks and recommended actions specific to generative AI.  
   https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf

3. **Adversarial Machine Learning: A Taxonomy and Terminology of Attacks and Mitigations (2025)** — AI attacks, attacker goals, and mitigation approaches.  
   https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-2e2025.pdf

4. **Reducing Risks Posed by Synthetic Content** — Content provenance, watermarking, detection, and transparency.  
   https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-4.pdf

5. **Secure Software Development Practices for Generative AI and Dual-Use Foundation Models: An SSDF Community Profile** — Secure development practices for generative AI systems.  
   https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/218/a/final

6. **NIST Privacy Framework: An Overview** — Privacy risk management and protecting personal information.  
   https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=930470
